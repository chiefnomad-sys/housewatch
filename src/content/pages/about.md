Housewatch covers the fire service for the people on the rig: career, volunteer, paid-on-call and wildland. Not the chief at the podium, not the vendor. You.

We cover three things.

- **Your paycheck.** Pay, contracts, overtime, staffing, pensions and schedules.
- **Your health.** Cancer, PFAS, cardiac, behavioral health, injuries, presumption laws and workers' comp.
- **The job.** Tactics, training, gear, apparatus, culture and the people in the firehouse.

## How we report

Housewatch reports from public records and published sources: contracts, arbitration awards, budgets, bills, court filings, studies and other outlets' reporting. We go to the primary document whenever there is one, and every story lists its sources at the bottom.

We don't run our own interviews. When we quote someone, the quote comes from a public source, and we say where it came from.

## The name

The housewatch is the desk at the front of the station, where whoever has the watch takes the calls, tracks the alerts and logs who comes and goes. That's the job we're trying to do for you.
