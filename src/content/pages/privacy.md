## What we collect

- **Housewatch Daily.** If you sign up, we keep your email address so we can send you the newsletter.
- **Messages.** If you use the contact form, we keep what you send us.

That's it. We don't run ad trackers on this site, and we don't sell or share your information.

## Stopping the newsletter

Every issue has an unsubscribe link. You can also use the [contact form](/contact/) to ask us to delete your email address.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top.
